A CORDIC + Kalman attitude coprocessor for TinyTapeout: one shift-add iterator shared across circular/linear CORDIC and Kalman microcode, with no hardware multiplier or reciprocal ROM. Turns gyro/accel/mag streams into roll, pitch, and yaw.
